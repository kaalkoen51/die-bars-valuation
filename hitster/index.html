Play Hitster without the board game

A song plays — you don't see what it is. Guess where it belongs on your timeline of release years. Get it right and you keep the card. First to the target number of cards wins.

1. Connect Spotify

Requires a Spotify Premium account (needed to play full songs in the browser) and a free Spotify Client ID. How do I get a Client ID?

  1. Go to the Spotify Developer Dashboard and log in.
  2. Click Create app. Give it any name & description.
  3. Set the Redirect URI to exactly:
  4. Under APIs used, tick Web Playback SDK. Save.
  5. Open the app's Settings and copy the Client ID into the box below.

📱 On a phone: open the Spotify app, play any song for a second so the app becomes active, then tap Refresh devices and pick your phone here. Audio plays from the Spotify app while you guess in this browser.

2. Choose the music

Pick a playlist that spans many decades — that's what makes the year-guessing fun. The songs become the deck of cards.

3. Players

Tokens are earned by naming a song's title & artist, and spent to steal a card when another player places it wrong.
